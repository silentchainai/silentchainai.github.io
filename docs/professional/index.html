What SILENTCHAIN Professional is

SILENTCHAIN Professional is a Burp Suite Professional extension that analyzes HTTP traffic intercepted by Burp using AI-assisted vulnerability analysis. It surfaces findings as native Burp Issues and in its own findings table, with findings mapped to the OWASP Top 10 and CWE identifiers. Professional goes beyond the free Community edition with active verification, using targeted payloads and testing techniques to confirm suspected vulnerabilities and produce evidence-backed findings.

When configured with a local AI provider such as Ollama, SILENTCHAIN Professional can perform AI analysis on your own hardware, keeping proxy traffic, credentials, and findings out of third-party AI services.

Requirements: Burp Suite Professional 2025.2 or later. See Requirements for supported platforms, runtimes, AI providers, and network requirements.

How it works

SILENTCHAIN Professional operates in two phases:

Phase 1 - Passive AI analysis

Analyzes in-scope HTTP traffic and surfaces findings as native Burp Issues plus its own findings table, classified against OWASP Top 10 / CWE Top 25. Untrusted traffic is wrapped in delimiters and passed through a best-effort PII / secret redaction pass before content reaches a cloud AI provider.

Phase 2 - Active verification

Off by default. When enabled, Professional actively tests eligible injection-class findings using curated payloads and AI-guided verification strategies. It can use WAF detection and fingerprinting to adapt testing when a target is actively blocking requests, and supports optional out-of-band checks for blind vulnerabilities. Findings that are successfully confirmed are promoted to Burp's Certain confidence level and include the evidence gathered during verification.

Only enable Phase 2 against targets you are authorized to test.

Features

  • 10 AI providers - Burp AI, Ollama, OpenAI, GPT-5.5, Claude, Claude Code, Gemini, OpenRouter, Z.ai, and Azure AI Foundry
  • OWASP Top 10 + CWE mapping - Findings are classified against OWASP Top 10 categories and mapped to CWE identifiers
  • Phase 2 active verification - 200+ curated payloads, AI-guided testing, WAF detection and fingerprinting across 24 types, and optional out-of-band testing
  • Sensitive-data sanitization - Best-effort PII and secret redaction before content is sent to a cloud AI provider
  • Burp integration - Context-menu actions in Proxy, Repeater, Target, and HTTP history, with findings rendered as native Burp Issues
  • Reporting - Export findings as HTML or CSV reports
  • Local AI - Use Ollama for AI analysis on your own hardware without sending analysis data to a third-party AI provider
