FEATURES

Built for Modern
Security Professionals

Every feature designed to enhance your workflow, from real-time analysis to intelligent reporting.

🧠

Multi-Provider AI

Up to 10 AI providers: Burp AI, Ollama, OpenAI, Claude, Claude Code, Gemini, and more. Context-aware vulnerability detection that goes beyond pattern matching. Use local models for air-gapped environments.

📚

OWASP Top 10 + CWE Mapping

Every finding is mapped to the OWASP Top 10 and a CWE identifier with a severity score, so each issue lands in a framework your team already reports against - no manual triage translation required.

🔗

Client-Ready Reports

Export a polished HTML advisory report with the exact request and response that proves each finding. Hand it straight to a client or a developer - evidence included, no rework needed.

⚡

Phase 2 Active Verification

AI-driven payload generation with 200+ curated OWASP payloads. WAF detection and fingerprinting across 24 WAF signatures. Out-of-band testing for XSS, SSRF, blind injection, RFI, and XXE.

🔒

Local-First & Private

Point SILENTCHAIN at a local Ollama model and analysis runs on your machine - no request, response, or finding is sent to a third-party AI service. Cloud providers are optional; sensitive data is redacted before any external call.

🎯

Runs Inside Burp Suite

Loads as a standard Burp extension and analyzes the traffic already flowing through your proxy - no separate scanner to deploy, no new workflow to learn. Passive by default, active when you choose.

3 Simple Steps
